Traffic Untie

Slide parked cars aside and open a route to the exit.

About the game

Traffic Untie is a puzzle set in a crowded parking lot. One target car needs to reach the exit on the right. Clear its lane by moving the other vehicles and finding a sequence of moves that opens the road.

Each vehicle slides along its own orientation: horizontal cars move left and right, while vertical cars move up and down. Cars cannot pass through one another, and the edges of the parking lot limit the available space. You may need to shift one vehicle before another can move far enough to clear the lane.

Drag the cars through the empty spaces and guide the target car out once its route is open. The level ends when that car leaves; you do not need to remove every vehicle from the parking lot.

How to play

  1. 1.
  2. Find the target car and the exit to its right.
  3. 2.
  4. Drag blocking vehicles along their row or column.
  5. 3.
  6. Clear the lane from the front of the target car to the exit.
  7. 4.
  8. Drag the target car out, or tap it when its route is clear.
