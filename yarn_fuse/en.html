Yarn Fuse

Drop yarn balls, merge matching pairs and build your best score.

About the game

Yarn Fuse is an endless puzzle with balls of yarn. Choose a position above the board and drop a ball so it touches another of the same kind. Matching pairs merge into the next, larger kind and add points to your score. A pair of the largest yarn balls disappears and also awards points.

Watch the queue of upcoming balls and leave room for them. Falling yarn balls collide, roll and stack on top of one another, so each drop changes the space available for the next move. Bring matching kinds together and keep the upper part of the board from becoming crowded.

Aim to earn more points and improve your best score. If yarn balls stay above the overflow boundary for too long, the run ends.

How to play

  1. 1.
  2. Press the board and move your finger left or right to aim.
  3. 2.
  4. Release over the board to drop the yarn ball.
  5. 3.
  6. Bring matching kinds into contact and plan around the upcoming queue.
  7. 4.
  8. Earn points while preventing sustained overflow at the top of the board.
