Block Outburst

Place block shapes, clear full lines and complete level objectives.

About the game

Block Outburst is a block-shape puzzle played on an 8×8 board. Drag shapes from the tray onto empty cells. A completely filled horizontal row or vertical column disappears, opening space for your next moves.

The tray holds three shapes. A new set arrives after all three have been placed, so leave room for different shapes as you plan their order. Each piece must fit fully inside the board without overlapping occupied cells.

In Adventure mode, complete the level objective: reach the required score or collect the requested gems by clearing lines that contain them. In Classic mode, place blocks and clear lines to build your score. The run ends when none of the remaining shapes can fit on the board. Choose where to place each shape, connect occupied areas into full lines and keep enough space open for the next set.
