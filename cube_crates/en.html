Cube Crates

Choose colored crates, collect matching cubes and open new paths.

About the game

Cube Crates is a puzzle built around colored cubes and a queue of crates. Tap an available crate to place it in a free work slot. Workers automatically collect cubes of the same color when an open path leads to them. Each crate has a capacity, and completing its delivery frees the slot for another selection.

Your goal is to clear the whole board. Removing cubes opens passages and exposes new areas, so the order in which you choose crates affects the moves that follow. Check the colors along reachable edges and the work slots you still have available. If the slots are occupied by jobs that cannot reach their matching cubes, progress can stop. Plan the queue and dismantle the board one delivery at a time.
