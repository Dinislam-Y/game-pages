Potion Unmix

Sort colorful potions until each bottle holds a single color.

About the game

Potion Unmix is a color sorting puzzle with layered potions. Different colors share the same bottles, and your task is to sort them until every filled bottle contains just one color. Pour the top layers, use empty bottles as temporary space and think through the order of your moves. A well planned pour can open the way to the next step, while a rushed move may take up valuable room. Solve puzzles of logic and attention in a calm setting.

How to play

  1. Click or tap the bottle you want to pour from, then select the receiving bottle.
  2. A continuous top group of one color can move into an empty bottle or onto the same color if there is enough room.
  3. You cannot pour onto a different color.
  4. A full bottle of a single color is already complete.
  5. Keep sorting until every nonempty bottle is full and contains only one color.
  6. If no legal pours remain, reconsider the sequence and restart the level.
