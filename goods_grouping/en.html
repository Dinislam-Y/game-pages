Goods Grouping

Match goods in groups of three and clear the shelves before time runs out.

About the game

Goods Grouping is a shelf sorting puzzle. Move an available front item into an empty space and bring three matching goods together within one shelf section. The matching group disappears, making room for your next moves. When the entire front of a shelf is empty, the goods behind it move forward.

Clear all the goods before the timer runs out. Keep track of empty spaces so you can rearrange items and prepare the next match. The layouts introduce chains, ice, hidden stock, moving conveyors and falling shelves. Clearing groups opens blocked spaces, while newly revealed goods change the choices available on the board. Look across the shelves and decide which group to complete first.

How to play

  1. 1.
  2. Drag an available front item into an empty unlocked position.
  3. 2.
  4. Place three matching items next to each other in one shelf section to clear them.
  5. 3.
  6. Empty the entire shelf front to reveal its back stock.
  7. 4.
  8. Clear all goods before the timer runs out.
